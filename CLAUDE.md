# When Machines Take the Wheel — article repo

Single-file article site: edit `index.html` directly (CSS top, article HTML middle, chart JS bottom). No build step. Preview: `python3 -m http.server` and open `index.html`.

Always follow the `article-writing` skill (in `.claude/skills/`) when touching prose, headlines, captions, or CTAs. Hard rules: no em dashes; no numbers in headlines; scope every quantitative claim; zero self-praise adjectives; compact CTAs.

All benchmark numbers must trace to the `e_commerce_env` repo (`benchmarks/` results). Do not invent or round numbers past what the data supports. Pre-publication open items are tracked in README.md.
